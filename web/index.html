How to get it into your world
  1. Tap Download add-on. Your phone saves a .mcaddon file.
  2. Open that file from your Downloads — Minecraft opens automatically and imports it.
  3. In your world or Realm settings, turn the Junk Bunch packs on (both of them).
  4. On PS5, join the Realm — it downloads the packs for you.
  5. Craft the summon item, hold L2 aiming at the ground, and your character appears.
  6. Hold L2 on them while holding the item to make them follow you.
